Second Brain

Second Brain is a personal, self-hosted assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps organise that person's tasks, mail, calendar and YouTube channel.

It is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not a public service and does not accept sign-ups.

Google account access

The app asks its owner for read-only access to Gmail, Google Calendar, YouTube and YouTube Analytics. The data is read on the owner's computer and stays there.
