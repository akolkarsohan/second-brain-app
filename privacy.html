Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Second Brain is a personal, self-hosted app used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app has read-only access to the owner's own Google data:

  • Gmail messages and metadata
  • Google Calendar events
  • YouTube channel, video and comment data
  • YouTube Analytics reports

How the data is used

The data is used only to give the owner summaries, task suggestions, meeting preparation and channel statistics. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored only on the owner's own computer and accounts.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is not used to train generalised AI or machine learning models.
  • No data from any other person's Google account is collected.

Limited Use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted by the owner on their own computer.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
